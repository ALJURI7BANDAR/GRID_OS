# ⚡ GRID_OS
### AI-Powered Smart Energy Management System

## 🌐 About the Project

GRID_OS is an individual project developed as part of the Kanz AI Hackathon.

The project explores the application of Artificial Intelligence in smart energy management, focusing on electricity demand forecasting, energy consumption analysis, and improving energy efficiency.

It aims to demonstrate how AI-driven insights can support smarter energy planning and contribute to a more sustainable energy future.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- ⚡ Forecast electricity demand.
- 📊 Analyze energy consumption patterns.
- 🔍 Identify peak electricity demand periods.
- 🤖 Explore AI-driven energy management solutions.
- 🌱 Promote energy efficiency and sustainability.

## 📊 Dashboard

The project includes a dashboard concept designed to present energy-related data and provide insights into electricity consumption and demand.
